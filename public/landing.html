How it works

From a bank statement to answers in three steps

1

Import a statement

Drop in a CSV from your bank or card. Ledgerline detects the columns, works out which rows are income and which are spending, and shows a preview before anything is saved.

  • Handles signed amounts or separate debit and credit columns
  • Skips duplicates, so importing the same file twice is safe
  • Sample bank and card files included to try
2

Everything sorts itself

Each transaction gets a category the moment it arrives. Fix one that's wrong and Ledgerline remembers.

  • Your own rules come first, then built-in keywords
  • A small on-device model learns from every correction
  • Nothing is sent anywhere to categorize
3

See it, then ask about it

Browse a month or a whole year, follow the money from income to savings, or simply ask the assistant in plain English.

  • Dashboard with spike warnings against your 3-month average
  • Cash-flow report from income to categories to savings
  • An assistant that answers from your own numbers
Features

Small on purpose, careful where it counts

Dashboard

Spending by month, by day, or across a full year, with a category breakdown and a warning when one runs well above your 3-month average.

Cash flow

One picture of the month: income flowing into categories and savings. On a phone it becomes a clear list of bars.

Smart categories

Rules you write, built-in keywords, then a classifier that learns from your corrections. An uncategorized queue keeps stragglers visible.

An assistant that checks the data

The built-in assistant is instant, offline and exact. Plug in a local Ollama or Claude model if you want more natural phrasing.

Private by design

Your records live in one SQLite file you control. There are no bank logins and no analytics, and no third party unless you turn on a hosted AI.

Measured, not assumed

The retrieval behind the assistant is tested against a hand-labelled 50-question eval, and the results, including the weak spots, are published.

Assistant

Ask it like you'd ask a friend who kept the books

It reads only your own transactions. Questions about totals, comparisons and savings are answered with numbers computed from your data, and search questions list the matching transactions.

  • What were my biggest purchases in August?
  • Where can I save?
  • Compare this month to last month
  • Which subscriptions look unused?
  • Show me my Shell charges

Try it in ten seconds

One click puts you in a private sandbox filled with 15 months of sample transactions. Import a CSV, change a category, ask the assistant a question. Everything is deleted after a day.

The demo assistant has no AI model: the model runs locally with Ollama, so online it answers common questions about the sample data with built-in rules. Copy the project below to use a real LLM.

The demo assistant runs on a cloud AI model with a small daily limit, so your questions and a summary of the sample data are sent to Anthropic's API.

It's a demo: please don't enter real financial details.
